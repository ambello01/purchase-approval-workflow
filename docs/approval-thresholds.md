# Approval Thresholds

| Purchase amount | Approver required |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Under 500       | Team lead         |
| 500 to 5,000    | Department manager|
| Over 5,000      | Finance director  |

Boundaries: 500 is routed to the department manager, 5,000 is routed to the department manager, and anything above 5,000 goes to the finance director. Amounts of zero or less are rejected as invalid.
